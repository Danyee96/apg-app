# Delivery handover and cash receiving

New deliveries use two stages. Super admin saves a goods handover with receiver, bilties, loading/Suzuki charges and discounts; no collection fields are present and the server rejects a cash amount on the handover endpoint. Operations submit the same handover through Pending delivery goods for super admin approval first. Approval creates the handover and its cash invoice. Existing deliveries and their previously recorded cash remain unchanged; no historical invoice or accounting backfill occurs.

Delivery Cash Receiving is super-admin-only. Its Delivery invoices view lists new handovers and payment status. Each bill has one row/card per bilty with its customer and Party to. Paid in full fills that bilty's outstanding amount; partial amounts can be entered, or No payment leaves it unpaid. The live feedback shows each customer's remaining balance. Payments for one bilty/customer never silently cover another's shortfall. Later collections use the same invoice. Overpayments are retained as customer credit.

The amount to collect is bilty amount + godown - advance - discount + agreed loading + Suzuki. Values are integer paisa; negative net amounts remain credits, rather than being clamped away. Customer and charge snapshots are saved when the handover is created. Before cash processing begins, editing the delivery can correct the invoice. Once processing begins, amounts, allocations and handover date are locked; receiver/contact/remarks corrections remain available without changing collections.

On the first confirmed collection (including an explicit zero-cash/on-account confirmation), the system posts a linked, balanced JV: each bilty's net amount is debited/credited to its selected customer, with the balancing entry in the dedicated Delivery charges clearing account (`SYS-DELIVERY-CLEARING`). The clearing account does not classify those charges as income: freight/payable allocation remains a separate accounting decision. Actual positive collections create linked RVs that debit the selected active Cash In Hand/bank account and credit the corresponding customers. Thus a short collection leaves the balance on that customer's actual ledger, and later linked collections reduce it. These vouchers appear in existing ledgers and financial reports. No manual voucher is needed for the same collection.

The Customer balances view shows delivery balances separately from unrelated historical/manual accounting entries. Before cash receiving is confirmed, it identifies amounts awaiting collection; those unconfirmed amounts have not yet been posted to the ledger. Each customer has a link to the full account ledger. Manual/unrelated account receipts do not automatically allocate against delivery invoices; use the linked collection screen for these deliveries.

Receipt history stores date, collector, cash account, amounts per bilty, notes and linked voucher. A request key prevents duplicate postings after network retries. A version fingerprint rejects stale collection forms; all invoice assessments, voucher lines, receipt lines and cumulative recorded cash are committed in one SQLite transaction. Only super admin can call the cash endpoints. No physical printer is selected by the app; cash statements use the existing A4/thermal preview choices and show saved collections, not unfinished inputs.
