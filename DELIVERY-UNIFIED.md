# Unified Delivery Goods

Delivery Goods now contains document search, entry, subsequent collections, delivery reports and customer balances. Old cash-receiving links open this workspace. Desktop has aligned bilty and service grids; mobile stacks them with bilty identifiers. Initial confirmed full bilty collection determines walk-in classification. Payment and cleared statuses are calculated independently, with positive debts never offset by another row or service credit.

## Accounting and migration

New deliveries assess the bilty/customer receivable when saved, even with zero payment. Loading and Suzuki use independent receivable and clearing control accounts, not the customer account. These controls do not assume a revenue classification. Zero cash creates no receipt. Each later payment appends immutable receipt lines, with independent dates; editing assessed charges creates adjustment journals. Request keys and invoice versions prevent retries or stale edits from duplicating transactions. Operator collections now post immediately on save, using the same receipt and ledger logic as super-admin collections. Delivery-create permission remains required for operators; viewers cannot collect. Payment approval is no longer part of the daily flow.

The user authorized reclassification of older outstanding service balances. Migration adds balanced adjustment journals removing those service debts from customer accounts and assigning service control accounts. Original historical ledger rows, vouchers, imported delivery source values and receipts remain unchanged. Where old records contain only combined loading/Suzuki cash, the outstanding amount stays explicitly labelled legacy combined service balance. No historical split is invented. A new explicitly categorized payment can transfer that amount from the legacy control to the chosen service control before collection. Reopening imported deliveries adopts their receipt history without posting original charges or cash again. Unknown original dates are labelled unrecorded rather than presented as known dates.

Migration phases are idempotent and tracked by `delivery_unified_version` (6). Later schema/view upgrades never repeat the historical adjustment. Existing print routes retain original delivery source amounts and receipt dates. Category overpayments remain credits in that category and cannot clear another category's outstanding debt. Existing supported overpayment warnings remain nonblocking.

## Delivery reports

Delivery Reports is an inner tab in Delivery Goods. Its Overview, Pending Payments, Partially Paid and Fully Paid views reuse the same permission-checked document actions. Counts, amount due, collected cash and customer/service balances aggregate the complete filtered result, independently of pagination. Payment breakdown uses current confirmed collections; pending operator cash is excluded until approval. Date filters refer to the delivery document date, not receipt dates or historical balances as of that day. Reports open in Single date mode for today in Asia/Karachi, and Clear filters restores today. Switching payment-status views keeps the selected day/range. Single-date mode uses equal inclusive start/end dates. Users can choose a different day or an explicit date range; explicit URL filters survive reload. Sort order is selected from a server-side whitelist. Report view/date/sort selections survive reload through URL parameters. Report reads do not post or modify accounting data.

## Operator cash policy change

Operator receipts post immediately through both unified delivery save and older cash/walk-in entry points. The former submission helpers delegate to receipt recording; no new approval rows are created. The Payment Approvals tab and old pending-cash links no longer lead to an approval queue. Historical submission records and original collector names remain intact. `settle_pending_delivery_cash()` is a deployment-only transition helper for any existing queue: it uses the original amounts/dates/account, stable receipt keys and a transaction per entry, preserving proposals and audit history. Invalid entries remain preserved and are reported rather than deleted. On deployment the live queue was checked before changing policy.

## Combined service collection input

The service grid uses one REC S AMT input per bilty for new cash. Both preview calculations and server posting allocate new cash to unpaid loading first, then Suzuki. Any supported overpayment remains Suzuki service credit; it never clears bilty/customer debt. Imported combined service accounts stay combined without guessing their original split. The entered date is assigned only to components receiving cash. Existing receipt history and independent service ledgers are preserved. The API continues to accept older explicitly split inputs, but rejects a combined amount alongside positive split amounts to prevent double counting.

## Previous customer balance in the bilty grid

Received to date is removed from the entry grid; saved receipts remain in Payment history. Previous Balance sums positive unpaid bilty/customer amounts from the customer's other delivery documents, excluding the entire current document and excluding service receivables. Total Receivable adds that previous balance to this row's remaining bilty amount after advances, discounts and saved receipts. Draft REC B AMT reduces the preview immediately; saving posts both current and older-bilty receipts atomically. Every row shows its customer's previous balance, but footer/customer totals count it once per distinct account. Loading or failed balance requests show an unavailable value rather than an invented zero; stale requests cannot replace newer customer selections. Existing collection permissions still apply. The combined collection flow below settles older balances on their original invoices, without reposting their charges.

## Collecting current and previous balances together

REC B AMT now covers the selected bilty first, then that customer's older positive bilty balances in delivery-date/document/item order. Service collections never pay customer debt. Each current row retains its own payment; only its excess is eligible for older documents. Repeated customers share one older-debt pool in previews and posting. Pay in full adds the previous customer balance once. Total Receivable displays the amount remaining after draft cash, and becomes zero when both current and prior balances are covered. Only cash beyond all of that customer's debts becomes supported credit on the entered bilty.

New UI requests explicitly opt in using `include_previous_balances` and a server-provided `previous_balance_version`. Previous-debt changes reject atomically with a refresh instruction. The original request key remains the replay guard. In one transaction, new receipts are appended against the original older delivery documents and cash/customer accounts; a `delivery_customer_collections` link makes those receipts visible in the collecting entry's Payment History and the cash-summary print totals. The immutable old receipts/charges and service ledgers are preserved. A previous-only payment creates no artificial zero receipt against an already-paid current bilty. Older clients without the opt-in retain their existing bilty-only allocation.
